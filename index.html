How to Play

Objective

Outmaneuver your opponent on a triangular grid. Win by eliminating your opponent through strategic tile destruction and pillar attacks.

Game Board

  • 48 triangular tiles arranged in a hexagonal pattern
  • Triangles point up or down alternately
  • Each tile has layers (health points)
  • Players start at opposite ends of the board

Movement

  • Players alternate turns
  • Click on a highlighted yellow tile to move there
  • When you leave a tile, it loses 1 layer
  • Tiles with 0 layers are destroyed and cannot be used
  • You cannot move onto pillars or your opponent's tile

Pillars

  • Blue/Green structures with their own layer count
  • When adjacent to a pillar, the "Knock Pillar" button appears
  • Knocking a pillar damages tiles in the opposite direction (shown in pink)
  • If your opponent is on a pink tile when you knock, they get pushed back
  • Pillars can be used strategically to trap opponents

Winning

  • Trap your opponent with no valid moves
  • Knock your opponent off the board with a pillar
  • Squish your opponent against a pillar

Tile Colors

  • Orange: 2 layers remaining
  • Red: 1 layer remaining
  • Dark: Destroyed (0 layers)
  • Yellow: Valid move
  • Pink: Will be hit if you knock pillar

About Trifecta

Trifecta is a turn-based strategic board game played on a unique triangular grid.

Game Design

Combining elements of positional strategy, resource management, and tactical warfare, players must carefully plan their moves to outmaneuver their opponent while managing destructible terrain and deadly pillars.

Features

  • Unique triangular grid system
  • Dynamic terrain destruction
  • Strategic pillar mechanics
  • 2-player local multiplayer
  • Simple yet deep gameplay
